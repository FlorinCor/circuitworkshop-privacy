Delete your data

CircuitWorkshop has no player accounts or game server. You can remove its saved data directly from your Android device.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Remove all local app data

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → CircuitWorkshop.
  2. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  3. Choose Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

Android labels vary by device. You can also uninstall CircuitWorkshop. These actions remove completed and unlocked levels, the selected level, circuit drafts, settings, tutorial and schematic preferences, the age-group choice where available, retained recovery records and locally stored advertising choices. Clear cache alone does not erase these records.

Removed local progress cannot be restored through a CircuitWorkshop account or game server. The app opts out of Android app backup. After clearing data, the app starts with fresh local saves. Versions with age selection ask for your age group again before checking Google's consent status and requesting ads.

Restart a single circuit

While editing a circuit, open More options → Restart board and confirm. This starts that circuit's construction again. Earned completion, other circuit drafts, settings, advertising choices and retained recovery records remain. Restart is not a complete data-deletion action.

Advertising data and support messages

Clearing local storage or uninstalling the app does not delete information already sent to Google or emails you sent to the developer. See the advertising section of the privacy policy for Google's policies and privacy controls.

For questions or requests about information you shared directly with the developer, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com. Describe your request without including unnecessary personal information. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase saves held only on your device.
